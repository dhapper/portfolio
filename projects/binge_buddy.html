Binge Buddy

TV Companion App

Binge Buddy is a TV companion app designed to enhance users' viewing experience by allowing them to search for shows and easily add them to their watchlist or watch history. This app empowers users to track their future viewing preferences and provides personalized recommendations based on their watch history. Utilizing the TVmaze API, Binge Buddy delivers real-time show data while leveraging Firebase for hosting, database management, and authentication. Users can create their own accounts to personalize their experience, and the app features a sleek user interface with an efficient dark/light mode implementation, ensuring a visually appealing and user-friendly experience.
